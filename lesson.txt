Formik manages:

* form values
* errors
* touched fields
* submission

Yup defines rules in a clean way.

## ✅ Form Validation

Ensures user input is correct before submission.

Examples:

* Email must be valid format
* Password must be at least 8 characters
* Required fields cannot be empty

## ✅ Authentication

Verifying a user’s identity (login/signup).

Examples:

* Login form → checks email & password
* Signup form → creates account

## ✅ Form Submission

Sending validated data to backend/API.
